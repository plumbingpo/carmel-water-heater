Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Each hour without hot water costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. For a salon, it empties the chairs. In a hotel, it means complaints at the front desk, and in a multifamily building it leads to tenant disputes. Carmel Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, get hot water flowing again, and document the entire call for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.
